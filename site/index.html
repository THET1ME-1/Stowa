Support

Stowa is a notes app that files things for you. You type into the line at the bottom, and the app adds a tag, sets a reminder, and picks a folder. The same line searches everything you saved.

Something broke, or you have a question? The quickest way is right in the app: Settings → Help → Report a problem, and your version and phone model are attached for you. Or by email:

support@stowa.work

In an email, include your Stowa version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and phone model, plus a screenshot if you can. We answer in English and Russian.

Where are my notes?

On your phone, as Markdown files. You chose the folder on first launch, and Settings → Catalog shows it. If you picked shared storage, Obsidian can open the same folder. On iPhone it is in the Files app, in the Stowa folder.

How do I move my notes to a new phone?

In Settings, tap the row with your vault name under Catalog. There, Save a backup packs all notes, folders, and files into one archive. Send it to the new phone and choose Restore from backup. The AI key is not included, so enter it again.

I forgot my passcode

We don't know it either: it is stored nowhere but in your memory. The passcode screen has a Forgot the code button. It removes the lock, but hidden folders are erased together with their notes and cannot be recovered. Regular notes stay.

Do I need AI?

No. Stowa sorts, searches, and sets reminders on its own, right on the phone. AI turns on only if you enter your own API key in Settings → AI sorting. You then pay your provider for requests, and we never see the key.

The edge spines disappeared

Spines are the strips at the screen edge that open pinned notes over any app. Xiaomi, Redmi, and POCO phones close Stowa when you swipe it away from recents, and the spines come back a few seconds later. If they don't, open Stowa once and check that autostart and display over other apps are allowed for it in your phone settings.

What does Stowa send over the internet?

The privacy policy covers it in detail. In short, we receive crash reports without note text (you can turn them off) and the support messages you send yourself.
