What's free

Stowa on a single device is free: the phone app, the stowa.work site and the desktop app, with every feature except the ones below. AI sorting runs on your own key and is paid to the service you chose.

What Stowa Sync gives you

  • sync of notes and files between your devices, encrypted on the device;
  • cloud storage for photos, videos, PDFs and other files in your notes;
  • version history: edits from all your devices, bringing back any version or deleted file. Every version is kept for a month, the last one of each day for up to a year, deleted files for a year.

Features marked "Soon" on the purchase screen will arrive in future releases and join the subscription at no extra cost. Until they ship, we don't promise them or charge for them separately.

Payment and renewal

The subscription comes for a month, 3 months or a year. You see the price and period before paying. The subscription belongs to your account: bought in one place, it works on every device where you sign in with the same email.

The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it. You can cancel anytime: card payments in Settings → Subscription in Stowa, Google Play purchases in Google Play, App Store purchases in your Apple ID settings. After you cancel, Stowa Sync keeps working until the paid period ends. The cloud copy is then kept for 30 more days before the server erases it. Everything stays on your devices.

Refunds

Refunds for Google Play and App Store purchases are handled by the store under its own rules. For card payments, write to us within 14 days of the charge: if you've barely used the subscription or it didn't work through our fault, we'll refund you the same way you paid.

Your notes

Everything you write and save belongs to you. We use your notes for nothing but syncing them and don't share them with anyone. You're responsible for what you store: don't keep anything in Stowa that breaks the law.

How the service runs

We work to keep sync running without breaks, but we can't promise that at all times: the network, the server or storage are sometimes unavailable. Your notes stay on your device meanwhile, and sync resumes on its own. Keep backups of what matters: in Stowa, that's Settings → Data.

We may close an account used to break the law or to attack the service. Otherwise your account and copies live until you delete them.

Deleting your account

You delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Cancel the renewal of a card subscription first. Google Play and App Store subscriptions are cancelled in the store.
